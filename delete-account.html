Delete Your OutAbout Account

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Delete from inside the app

Open OutAbout and go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Delete by email

If you no longer have OutAbout installed, email support@slowma.ai from the address you signed up with, using the subject line “Account deletion request”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Guest accounts have no email address attached, so we have no way to find or verify one from outside the app. If you used OutAbout as a guest, delete the account from within the app before uninstalling.

What deletion removes

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and email address
  • Your saved location
  • Your activities, categories and condition preferences
  • Your activity history and outcome records
  • Your push notification registration

De-identified, aggregated records that can no longer be linked to you are retained, as described in Section 4 of our Privacy Policy.

Calendar events that OutAbout added to your device calendar are not removed, because they live on your device and in your own calendar account, not on our servers. Delete them in your calendar app if you no longer want them.
