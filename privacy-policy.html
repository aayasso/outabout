Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

OutAbout ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the OutAbout mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information directly from you:

  • Email address – used for authentication and account management
  • Location data – your approximate location, used to fetch local weather conditions
  • Activity data – the activities and weather preferences you create within the app
  • Device token – used to deliver push notifications to your device

You can also use OutAbout without providing an email address, by continuing as a guest. Guest accounts collect no email address and cannot be recovered — if you sign out of or delete a guest account, its data cannot be restored.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the OutAbout service
  • To deliver weather-based activity notifications
  • To manage your account and authenticate you
  • To respond to your support inquiries
  • To send administrative information such as account updates
  • To evaluate and improve the app experience
  • To identify usage trends and protect the security of our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following third-party services that are necessary to operate OutAbout:

  • Supabase – database and authentication infrastructure
  • Tomorrow.io – weather data provider (receives your location coordinates)
  • OneSignal – push notification delivery

4. De-Identified Data

We produce de-identified, aggregated records of how weather conditions relate to activity behavior. These records contain no email address, no account identifier, and none of the free text you entered, and they are generalized so that they cannot reasonably be linked back to you or to your device.

We may license these de-identified, aggregated records to third parties, including for use in training machine learning and artificial intelligence models. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not license any data from which you can be identified.

Because de-identified records are no longer linked to you, they are not removed when you delete your account. See Section 5 for what account deletion does remove.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems; de-identified records that cannot be linked to you may be retained for analytics. See Section 4.

6. Push Notifications

OutAbout sends push notifications to alert you when weather conditions match your saved activities. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Update or correct your information within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings

8. Children's Privacy

OutAbout is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@slowma.ai.
